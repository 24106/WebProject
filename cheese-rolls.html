Cheese Rolls

Traditional New Zealand Cheese Rolls

A classic snack from New Zealand with a warm, cheesy filling wrapped in crispy bread.

Serves 20
Prep Time 20 mins
Cook Time 15 mins

What You'll Need

Ingredients

Cheese Filling

  • 1 can of reduced cream
  • 1 packet of onion soup mix
  • 1 cup of grated cheese

Bread & Assembly

  • 1 loaf of sandwich bread
  • Butter or margarine

Time To Cook

Method

Make the Cheese Filling

01

Mix the reduced cream and onion soup together in a bowl.

02

Add the grated cheese and mix until everything is well combined.

Prepare the Bread

01

If you don't like crusts, carefully cut the crusts off each slice of bread.

02

Spread each slice of bread with butter or margarine.

Roll the Cheese Rolls

01

Spread a large spoonful of the cheese mixture over ⅔ of the unbuttered side of bread.

02

Roll the bread up tightly, making sure the filling stays inside.

03

Place the completed cheese rolls onto a baking tray.

Bake the Cheese Rolls

01

Preheat the oven to 180°C.

02

Bake the cheese rolls for 12 to 15 minutes, or until golden brown.

03

Allow the cheese rolls to cool for 5 to 10 minutes before serving.
