About Chloe's Recipes

Welcome to Chloe's Recipes, a simple and organised website designed to to make cooking easier and more enjoyable.

This website has many different recipes, including snacks, main meals and sweet treats. Each recipe includes ingredients, instructions, images and credits to help the user follow along and discover something new to cook, while giving rightful credits to who made the recipe.

Simple

Recipes are presented clearly so users can easily find the information they need.

Creative

Explore different dishes and discover new ideas for cooking and baking.

Easy to Explore

Recipes are organised into catergories to make finding something to cook easy.
