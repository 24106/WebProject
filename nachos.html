Nachos

Crispy layered nachos loaded with savoury beef

A loaded nacho dish with seasoned mince, melted cheese, and crunchy chips.

Serves 8-12
Prep Time 20 mins
Cook Time 15 mins

What You'll Need

Ingredients

Beef

  • 1 Tbsp olive oil
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • ½ onion, finely chopped
  • 500g beef mince
  • 2 Tbsp tomato paste
  • 2 Tbsp water

Nachos Seasoning

  • 1 tsp onion powder
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 2 tsp ground cumin
  • 2 tsp paprika
  • ¼ tsp cayenne pepper

Toppings

  • 400g corn chips
  • 2 cups grated cheese
  • Guacamole
  • Sour cream

Time To Cook

Method

Prepare the Beef

01

Heat the olive oil in a large frying pan over high heat. Add the garlic and onion and cook until softened.

02

Add the beef mince and cook, breaking it apart with a wooden spoon until browned.

03

Add the onion powder, oregano, salt, cumin, paprika, cayenne pepper, tomato paste, and water.

04

Stir well and cook for a further 2 minutes, then remove from the heat.

Layer the Nachos

01

Preheat the oven to 180°C and prepare a large baking tray.

02

Spread half of the corn chips evenly across the tray.

03

Top with half of the cooked beef mixture and half of the grated cheese.

04

Repeat with the remaining corn chips, beef mixture, and grated cheese to create a second layer.

Bake and Serve

01

Bake for approximately 15 minutes, or until the cheese has melted and the nachos are heated through.

02

Remove from the oven and serve immediately while hot and the corn chips are still crispy.

03

Serve with guacamole and sour cream on the side.
