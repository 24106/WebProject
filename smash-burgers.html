Smash Burgers

Smash Burgers with Pickles and Mayo

Juicy double-patty smash burgers with fresh toppings and toasted broiche buns.

Serves 4
Prep Time 30 mins
Cook Time 5 mins

What You'll Need

Ingredients

Smash Burgers

  • 800g of beef mince
  • Salt
  • 2 Tbsp oil
  • Black pepper
  • 8 slices of cheese

Burger Sauce

  • ½ cup mayonnaise
  • 10 Pickles, sliced
  • 2 tsp mustard
  • 1 Tbsp tomato sauce
  • 1 Tbsp pickle brine
  • ½ tsp garlic powder
  • ½ tsp onion powder
  • ½ tsp smoked paprika
  • ½ tsp salt

Burger Assembly

  • 4 brioche burger buns, halved and toasted
  • Lettuce
  • 2 tomatoes, sliced
  • 1 red onion, sliced
  • Pickles
