Tiramisu

Coffee-soaked layers, rich mascarpone cream.

Coffee-soaked ladyfingers layered with creamy mascarpone, dusted with cocoa powder.

Serves 12
Prep Time 45 mins
Cook Time 0 mins

What You'll Need

Ingredients

Espresso Mixture

  • 1 cup (250ml) very strong prepared espresso, warm or room temperature
  • 40-45 ladyfingers

Mascarpone Cream

  • 473ml (450g) mascarpone, cold
  • 2 Tbsp (30ml) dark rum
  • 4 large pasturized eggs, separated
  • ½ cup (100g) granulated sugar
  • 2 cups (480ml) heavy cream
  • ½ tsp vanilla extract
  • ⅛ tsp salt

For Topping

  • Unsweetened natural or Dutch-process cocoa powder

Time To Make

Method

Prepare the Ladyfingers

01

Whisk the espresso in a shallow bowl and set aside.

02

Quickly dip each side of half of the ladyfinger into the espresso. Do not soak them for too long or they will become soggy.

03

Arrange the dipped ladyfingers in the bottom of a 23x33-cm baking dish, creating one even layer.

04

Cut some ladyfingers if needed to fill any empty spaces. Set the remaining ladyfingers and espresso aside.

Make the Mascarpone Cream

01

Beat the mascarpone in a large bowl for a few seconds until smooth. Do not overmix.

02

Separate the eggs, placing the yolks and whites in separate bowls.

03

Place the egg yolks and half of the sugar in a heatproof bowl over a saucepan of simmering water. Whisk for about 5 minutes until light and foamy.

04

Remove the yolk mixture from the heat and beat it into the mascarpone until combined.

05

Whip the heavy cream and vanilla extract until medium peaks form.

06

Gently fold the whipped cream into the mascarpone mixture until evenly combined.

07

Beat the egg whites and salt until foamy. Slowly add the remaining sugar and continue beating until stiff peaks form.

08

Gently fold the whipped egg whites into the mascarpone cream until smooth and fluffy.

Build the Tiramisu

01

Spread half of the mascarpone cream evenly over the first layer of ladyfingers.

02

Quickly dip the remaining ladyfingers into the espresso mixture.

03

Arrange the remaining ladyfingers over the mascarpone cream to create a second layer.

04

Spread the remaining mascarpone cream evenly over the second layer of ladyfingers.

05

Refrigerate the tiramisu uncovered for 2 to 3 hours.

Finish and Serve

01

Sift a generous layer of unsweetened cocoa powder evenly over the top of the tiramisu.

02

Cover the dish with plastic wrap or aluminium foil and refrigerate for at least 8 to 9 hours, preferably overnight.

03

When ready to serve, cut the chilled tiramisu into square portions using a sharp knife.

04

Wipe the knife clean between cuts and carefully remove each portion with a serving spatula.
